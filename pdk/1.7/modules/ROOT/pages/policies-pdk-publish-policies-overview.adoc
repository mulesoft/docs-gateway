= Uploading Custom Policies to Exchange
ifndef::env-site,env-github[]
include::_attributes.adoc[]
endif::[]
:imagesdir: ../assets/images

During the custom policy development cycle, you can upload your policy to Anypoint Exchange to create and manage policy assets, control versions, and distribute policies to other users or gateways. For more information about Exchange, see xref:exchange::index.adoc[].

The steps and commands differ depending on whether your policy is a unified-model or split-model project. Choose the guide that matches your project:

* xref:policies-pdk-publish-policies.adoc[Uploading a Unified-Model Policy to Exchange]
* xref:policies-pdk-publish-policies-split.adoc[Uploading a Split-Model Policy to Exchange]

After you upload your policy to Exchange, see xref:policies-pdk-apply-policies.adoc[] to apply the policy to your APIs.
