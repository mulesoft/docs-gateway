Flex Gateway supports high availability (HA) and disaster recovery (DR) through managed deployments on CloudHub 2.0 and self-managed multi-region deployments. Choose the topic that matches your deployment type:

* xref:flex-architecture-managed-dr-ha.adoc[Managed Flex Gateway HA and DR] — CloudHub 2.0 manages replica deployment, availability zones, and failover for Managed Flex Gateway.
* xref:flex-architecture-multi-region.adoc[Self-Managed Multi-Region HA and DR] — Deploy your self-managed Flex Gateway across multiple regions for latency reduction and live failover.
