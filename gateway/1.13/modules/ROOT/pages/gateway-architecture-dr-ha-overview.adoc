Omni Gateway supports high availability (HA) and disaster recovery (DR) through managed deployments on CloudHub 2.0 and self-managed multi-region deployments. Choose the topic that matches your deployment type:

* xref:gateway-architecture-managed-dr-ha.adoc[] — CloudHub 2.0 manages replica deployment, availability zones, and failover for Managed Omni Gateway.
* xref:gateway-architecture-multi-region.adoc[] — Deploy your self-managed Omni Gateway across multiple regions for latency reduction and live failover.
