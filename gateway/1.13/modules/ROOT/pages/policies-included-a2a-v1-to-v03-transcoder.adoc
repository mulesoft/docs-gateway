== Summary

The A2A V1 to v0.3 Transcoder policy lets A2A v1.0 JSON-RPC clients consume A2A v0.3 servers by transcoding requests, responses, errors, SSE streams, and agent cards on the wire. This policy enables compatibility between v1.0 clients and legacy v0.3 backend agents.
