== Summary

The A2A v1 to v0.3 Transcoder policy enables A2A v1.0 JSON-RPC clients to communicate with A2A v0.3 servers by transcoding protocol messages on the wire. This policy converts requests, responses, errors, SSE streams, and agent cards between the two protocol versions, allowing newer clients to consume legacy A2A v0.3 backends without modification.
